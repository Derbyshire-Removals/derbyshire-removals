## 6. Booking, Cancellation and Date Changes

A provisional booking does not reserve a removal date. If another customer requests the same date, we will give the first customer a stated opportunity to secure it by accepting our estimate and paying a 50% deposit. If the deposit is not paid by the deadline given, we may release the date.

Once the deposit is paid, we may turn away other work and reserve staff, vehicles and resources for that date.

The following cancellation or postponement charges will normally apply:

- **11 working days or more:** no charge
- **8 to 10 working days:** 25% of the removal charge
- **4 to 7 working days:** 50% of the removal charge
- **3 working days or less:** 100% of the removal charge

However, if we have turned away another customer and cannot replace the original booking, we may retain up to the full 50% deposit, even where the above scale would result in a lower charge.

Any request to change the agreed removal date will be treated as a postponement and will be subject to the cancellation charges set out above.
